I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Professor Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
